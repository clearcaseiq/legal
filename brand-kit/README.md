## Read this before using anything

**There is no single agreed identity.** Four different marks are currently in
production:

| Where | What it depicts |
| --- | --- |
| App header and footer (`logos/clearcaseiq-mark.svg`) | Navy shield, gold scales, magnifying lens with check |
| Marketing lockup (`logos/clearcaseiq-logo-*.png`) | Same concept, different and more detailed illustration, plus tagline |
| Social card (`social/clearcaseiq-og-card.png`) | Bright blue shield with a "C", no scales, no gold, different wordmark |
| Favicon and app icons (`icons/`, `mobile/`) | Navy rounded square, white document, gold check |

Reconciling these into one system is the obvious first piece of work for
whoever receives this kit. Note also that the raster lockup sets the wordmark in
a geometric sans, while the live product sets it in Fraunces, a serif.

A spelling error in the tagline, `TRANSEORMED` for `TRANSFORMED`, was repaired
on 1 September 2026. Every file here carries the corrected wording.

## What does not exist

No brand guidelines predate this document. There is no photography library, no
illustration set, no video or motion assets, and no previous advertising
creative. The social card is the only designed marketing artefact.

## Not for external use

Do not share anything from `api/uploads/` in the main repository. That directory
holds claimant-uploaded evidence, including photographs and video of injuries,
and is confidential.
